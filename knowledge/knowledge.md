Generated by `agent/knowledge_build.py` from the raw CSV files. Every number is computed, none typed by hand.

## Boundaries (what the data cannot support)

- **B-COV** [A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, P, X] The flow data covers 2026-06-10 05:00 to 2026-10-01 00:45 at 15-minute grain for 167 stations on the lines U1, U2, U3, U5, U6, U7, U8, U9 (there is no U4 data). Dates outside this window cannot be answered from data; only clearly labelled scenarios are possible. _(source: flows*.csv, stations_with_ubahn.csv)_
- **B-CAP** [A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, P, X] There is NO platform-capacity, headway, timetable or train-load data. 'Safe platform capacity' therefore cannot be measured or quoted; answers must say so and use a relative proxy (each station's own busiest-5% level, or predicted load) instead. _(source: dataset schema)_
- **B-EVT** [A, P, X] The events file has 457 events (182 with a venue name) from 2026-06-10 to 2026-09-30. Events carry a venue name and address but NO station key or coordinates, and attendance is an organiser estimate. The file contains 4 InnoTrans/Messe events. _(source: berlin_events*.csv)_
- **B-SIM** [A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, P] The passenger flows are simulated. 17711 readings equal exactly 500 (a clipping artefact, 1.2% of readings) although values up to 3000 exist. _(source: flows*.csv)_
- **B-CLS** [C, H] There are 30 recorded closures (reasons: safety inspection x10, switch replacement x7, track maintenance x6, signal upgrades x4, power system maintenance x3). They are simulated maintenance closures; closed stations read 0 and neighbouring stations show no measurable extra load. _(source: closures*.csv)_
- **B-OD** [H, G, X] The data has no origin-destination flows or route choices: which alternative routes passengers prefer, and why two stations are coupled, cannot be measured — only suggested. _(source: dataset schema)_
- **B-ASSUME** [C] How many passengers divert around a closure, and to which stations, is ASSUMED (diversion shares 25/50/75 %, nearest open stations). Closure pressure is a model-based scenario, never a measurement. _(source: ml/disruption.py)_
- **B-NAME** [A, P] The events file names the arena 'Uber Arena'; operators may say 'Mercedes-Benz Arena' (its earlier name). They are treated as the same venue — an assumption to be stated. _(source: berlin_events*.csv)_
- **B-EN** [E] Energy is daily MWh per line only; there is no rolling-stock, timetable or route-length data, so energy explanations are limited to ridership evidence. _(source: energy_consumption*.csv)_
- **B-NEW** [A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, P, X] A new dataset (2026-09-22 to 2026-10-01) is provided on the final day; loaders merge every matching file (dedupe on timestamp) and coverage is read from the data. _(source: dashboard/utils/data_loader.py)_
- **Q-NORMAL** [A, B, C, D, P] 'Normal' flow is defined by the quality database: for every station, time of day and day type the flow that remains after removing event / closure windows and the effect of weather (typical weather). Boundaries per station, day type and hour (p05-p95 of clean slots, highest value ever observed, hard ceiling = 1.5 x that maximum) are checked by the Inspector; a passenger figure above a station's hard ceiling is impossible in this dataset. _(source: data/quality (ml/quality_db.py))_
- **Q-CEILING** [A, C, P] The highest passenger flow ever observed at any station is 3000 per 15 minutes (Spichernstr.); the median station maximum is 502. A predicted or claimed load above 1.5 x a station's own maximum is treated as impossible; above 1.15 x as extreme. _(source: data/quality station_bounds)_
- **Q-OUTAGE** [B, C, H] 2061 readings (0.4% of the slots that normally carry >= 100) are exactly 0 outside any event or closure window: sensor gaps / outages; a claimed zero flow is not proof of a closure at the 15-minute grain the log residual has a standard deviation of 1.19: single slots are noisy, judge patterns over hours, not one slot _(source: data/quality outages)_
- **Q-TESTSHIFT** [A, B, C, D, P] the test split runs +0.10 (log) above the training-normal: the held-out days are busier than the training normal (InnoTrans week / trend), judge them against test-window boundaries too _(source: data/quality meta)_

## Insights (how answers must be written)

- **I-RAIN** [B, P] Rain (> 0.5 mm per 15 min, 419 slots) lifts total network flow to x1.27 of the dry value for the same weekday and time, and the number of stations pinned at 500 rises from 1.9 to 2.9 per slot. _(source: weather_data*.csv, flows*.csv)_
- **I-P-SKILL** [P] Ranking stations by the predicted busy-slot LOAD has skill: over 8 replay days the predicted top-3 stations had a mean observed peak of 1671 per 15 min versus 529 for the average station, Spearman 0.44. Ranking by the probability of exceeding a station's own p95 has NO skill (Spearman -0.05): those exceedances are noise-driven. Load, not p95-exceedance, is the ranking to use. _(source: knowledge/validation.json (evaluation/validate_pressure.py))_
- **I-ML** [C, P, D] TabPFN demand model on held-out days: MAE 74.62 vs 77.87 for the naive station-slot mean, RMSE 135.61 vs 135.05; pinball-loss skill +0.5%. It adds context-conditioned probabilities more than a different decision. _(source: ml/output/disruption_baseline_report.json)_
- **Q-WEATHER** [B, D, P] Weather explains very little of the flow: weather explains 0.5% of the variance of the total: the flows are simulated with little weather signal Median station effects: rain now/prev. hour 16.38 %, +5 degC -8.14 %, school holiday weekday 0.0 %. _(source: data/quality coefficients)_
