# Hackathon Questions for Testing the Solution

The following list of questions will be used to test your conversation agent to understand and explain passenger flow based on the city, operational, and environmental context, during the final day of evaluation. 

1. There is a System Of A Down concert on July 8th. What will the passenger flow look like at the neighboring stations and what measures should we take in operational sense?
2. Give me an example of a passenger flow peak caused by bad weather in the week of July 13-19 and provide additional information like the time and station where the peak took place.
3. Line U3 is suspended on a section between Spichernstrasse and Oskar-Helene-Heim stations. What is the reason behind this closure and how long will it last? How should the passengers be rerouted, which stations would become overloaded, and where should additional staff be deployed?
4. At what time does the commute flow peak at Adenauerplatz station usually take place? Does it exceed the mean commute peak value across all stations?
5. Identify three passenger-flow anomalies that cannot be explained by station closures on July 19th. Determine the most likely root causes using all of the available data.
